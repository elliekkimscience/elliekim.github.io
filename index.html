Research log

Three years at the bench

Since 2023, ongoing work at the University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences tracing how environmental toxicants — PFOA and arsenic — affect cancer cell lines, moving from single-compound assays toward combined-exposure mechanisms.

2023 – 2024
Project 01 · Pancreatic cancer cell lines

PFOA toxicity in human pancreatic cancer cells

Quantified the cytotoxic effects of PFOA using MTT assays, Oil Red O staining, and spectrophotometric analysis to characterize dose-dependent cell response.

2024 – 2025
Project 02 · Pancreatic cancer cell lines

Arsenic-induced gene expression changes

Analyzed arsenic-driven changes in gene expression in human pancreatic cancer cells using qRT-PCR, pairing lab work with data visualization to communicate findings.

2025 – Present
Project 03 · HepG2 liver cancer cells

PFOA & arsenic synergistic toxicity

Investigating the combined toxicity of PFOA and arsenic on HepG2 liver cancer cell lines to characterize synergistic mechanisms of harm.
