01 · Research

At the bench since 2023.

Independent research at the University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences focuses on environmental toxicology, cancer biology, and mechanisms of disease — supported by hands-on molecular and cellular techniques.

Current project

Therapeutic Prevention of PFOA-Induced Hepatic Steatosis

2025 — Present

Investigating the protective mechanism of soybean-derived genistein against PFOA-induced lipid accumulation in HepG2 liver models, with lipid deposition assessed through Oil Red O staining and spectrophotometric quantification.

HepG2PFOAGenisteinOil Red OSpectrophotometry
2023 — 2025

Pancreatic Oncogenesis Driven by Arsenic & PFAS Exposure

Analyzed physiological shifts and gene-expression changes in human pancreatic cancer cell lines and quantified cytotoxicity and transcriptional dysregulation using MTT assays and qRT-PCR.

Methods & communication

Mammalian cell culture · qRT-PCR · MTT assays · Oil Red O · spectrophotometry · immunoblotting · quantitative data analysis · experimental design · poster presentation.
